LEAD 01
│ TITLE: The Missile That Wasn't a Copy
│ BEAT: Military technology · Korean Peninsula · Ukraine conflict
│ PRIORITY: HIGH
│
│ The DPRK missiles recovered from Ukrainian strike sites are indigenous
│ North Korean designs. That single technical finding rewrites the risk
│ profile for every air defense system the West has sold or promised to
│ Ukraine — and it rewrites it in real time, with active warfare as the
│ test environment. Who has the debris? Who has analyzed it? What does
│ Kyiv's technical intelligence apparatus look like, and which Western
│ partners are inside that loop? The story is not the missile. The story
│ is what the missile proves about North Korean design capability and who
│ is now paying attention to that proof.
│
│ FEED PROMPT: Map the chain of custody for recovered DPRK missile debris
│ in Ukraine — from strike site to technical analysis to Western intelligence
│ sharing. Identify which NATO member states have received technical briefings
│ and whether that intelligence has reached South Korean or Japanese defense
│ ministries. Cross-reference with any South Korean MoD statements issued
│ after March 2026 referencing "new ballistic threat profiles."
