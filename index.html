Brownsville Border Crossing Sees Estimated Wait Times of Up to 80 Minutes for Passenger Vehicles

What Happened
U.S. Customs and Border Protection posted an estimated 80-minute wait for standard passenger vehicles alongside a 5-minute wait for standard commercial vehicles at the Brownsville Veterans International port of entry (port 535502) at 6:00 p.m. CST on Nov. 11, 2026 .
Primary Evidence
Port 535502 (Brownsville, Veterans International) reported a 5-minute wait for standard commercial lanes (2 of 4 lanes open, RFID) at 6:00 p.m. CST on 11/11/2026. Tier 1 primary bwt.cbp.gov
Why It Matters
A U.S. Customs and Border Protection feed posted a 75-minute gap between passenger and commercial vehicle wait times at the Veterans International crossing in Brownsville on Nov. 11, 2026.
