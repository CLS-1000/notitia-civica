# Editorial Standard

Notitia Civica publishes public-interest reporting from public signals.

Every piece should separate:

1. Sourced facts
2. Observable patterns
3. Analytical judgments
4. Unanswered questions
5. What to watch next

## Core Standard

A report should answer:

1. What happened?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What evidence supports it?
4. What remains uncertain?
5. What should readers watch next?

## Publication Rule

A pattern is not proof of intent.

Similarity is not proof of coordination. Funding is not proof of control. Silence is not proof of suppression. These are signals that may warrant further reporting.
