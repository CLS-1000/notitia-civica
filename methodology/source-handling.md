# Source Handling

Notitia Civica relies on public sources, including:

- public records
- official statements
- public reporting
- filings and disclosures
- court records
- legislative records
- regulatory comments
- open datasets
- archived public material

## Source Rule

Every major claim should be traceable to a source, an observed pattern, or a clearly labeled analytical judgment.

## No Private Access Claim

Notitia Civica does not claim classified information, privileged access, or private intelligence.
